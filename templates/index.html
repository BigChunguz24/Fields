About

Welcome to my website. On this website, we worship cats. Not in the metaphorical sense. We have chosen cats as our rightful overlords, and we are merely here to serve them. Please take a moment to admire His magnificence as you pay your respects to Him. Bow before the pixels. Offer Him your snacks. Do not question His infinite wisdom. If Cooper does not approve of your offering, you will know.
